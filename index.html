About KOS

Serving the Market since 1975

KOS has been part of Kensington Market since 1975 — a neighbourhood restaurant and bar on Bellevue Avenue where regulars and newcomers feel equally at home. Our kitchen serves breakfast all day — eggs any style, benedicts, omelettes, pancakes and French toast — alongside homemade burgers, sandwiches, salads and hearty lunch plates.

When the sun comes out, grab a seat on our huge patio. The bar opens at 9 am with brunch cocktails, and on weekdays from 4 to 8 pm our happy hour pairs a favourite dish with a pint.
